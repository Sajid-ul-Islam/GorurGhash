# Brand Analysis: Gorur Ghash (গরুর ঘাস)

## 1. Brand Identity & Positioning
* **Brand Name**: Gorur Ghash (Bengali for "Cow's Grass", a playful Bengali idiom that connotes quirky, irreverent authenticity).
* **Tagline / Philosophy**: *"Gorur Ghash is a fashion label that blends bold design with a sense of humor. We create pieces that make a statement, on the street, in a room, or in your mirror."*
* **Origin**: Started inside a bedroom in Dhaka, Bangladesh, growing into a globally recognized Bangladeshi lifestyle and streetwear brand shipping worldwide and expanding operations into North America (US).
* **Core Differentiator**: Everyday streetwear featuring vibrant nostalgia, pop-culture wit, quirky local cultural idioms, relaxed/oversized unisex silhouettes, and high-spec textiles (waffle weaves, heavy French terry, diamond knits, canvas chore fabrics, embroidery).

---

## 2. Target Audience & Demographics
* **Primary Demographics**: Gen-Z and Millennials (aged 16–35) across urban Bangladesh (Dhaka, Chittagong, Sylhet, etc.) and the global Bangladeshi diaspora (US, UK, Canada, Australia).
* **Psychographics**:
  * Fashion-forward youth who value self-expression, internet culture, graphic storytelling, and street fashion over traditional formalwear.
  * Shoppers looking for authentic, home-grown contemporary apparel that is ethically crafted and durable.
  * Mobile-first buyers accustomed to instant purchasing, bKash/Nagad mobile payments, and Cash on Delivery (COD).

---

## 3. Visual Identity & Color System
* **Primary Brand Accent**: `#0170B9` — Electric Ocean Blue (energetic, modern, confident).
* **Secondary / Contrast Colors**:
  * Rich Charcoal / Onyx: `#1A1A1A` / `#222222` (streetwear anchor, primary typography).
  * Crisp White / Pure Light: `#FFFFFF` (clean canvas, high-contrast product backdrop).
  * Warm Sand / Off-White: `#F5F5F0` / `#FAFAFA` (relaxed editorial look).
  * Flash / Sale Accent: `#CF2E2E` (vivid red for discounts, countdowns, hot items).
  * Mustard Amber Accent: `#FBDD02` (playful tag highlight).
* **Iconography & Badging**: Clean line icons, bold rounded pill tags (`NEW`, `SALE`, `OVERSIZED`, `UNISEX`).
* **Typography Hierarchy**:
  * Display / Headers: Modern geometric sans-serif (Poppins / Futura-style heavy bold headings).
  * Body / UI: Clean legible sans-serif (Inter / System font weights 400, 500, 600, 700).

## 4. Product Catalog Structure & Taxonomy
1. **Men / Unisex Tops**:

2. **Bottoms**:

   * Barrel Pants.

   * Heavyweight Carpenter Corduroy & Denim Pants.
3. **Outerwear & Jackets**:
   * Boxy Canvas Jackets.

4. **Accessories & Lifestyle**:
   * Structured Baseball Caps & Embroidered Bucket Hats.
   * Beaded & Cord Bracelets.
   * Digital Gift Cards (৳500 - ৳5000).

---

## 5. Pricing & Local Commercial Logic
* **Currency**: Bangladeshi Taka (`৳` / BDT).
* **Price Points**:
  * Basic & Graphic Tees: ৳850 – ৳1,250
  * Knits & Henleys: ৳900 – ৳1,450
  * Boxy & Crochet Shirts: ৳1,400 – ৳1,950
  * Cargo & Relaxed Trousers: ৳1,500 – ৳2,200
  * Jackets & Bombers: ৳2,200 – ৳2,800
* **Logistics & Delivery Expectations**:
  * Nationwide Cash on Delivery (COD) across all 64 districts of Bangladesh.
  * Inside Dhaka: 1–2 business days (standard charge ৳70).
  * Outside Dhaka: 4–5 business days (standard charge ৳130).
  * Urgent/Express Delivery: via on-demand parcel couriers (e.g., Pathao Parcel).
  * Trial-on-Spot & 48-Hour Exchange Policy: Customers can inspect/try on delivery; exchanges/store credits honored within 48 hours.
